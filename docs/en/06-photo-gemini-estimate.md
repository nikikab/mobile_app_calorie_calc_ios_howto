# 6. Food photo: Gemini estimate

Gemini estimates the meal from the photo: name, grams, calories and macros.

**Prerequisites:** A Gemini API key entered in Settings (see scenario 10) and your agreement to send photos. While a key is set, the photo is sent to Google on every shot.

## Steps

1. Take a photo of the food or choose one. The first time, an alert asks **Send to Google Gemini?**; read it and tap **Agree and continue**. With **Not now** nothing is sent.

2. After a few seconds the Gemini estimate appears under the photo, one card per food with its ingredients.

   ![Food photo: Gemini estimate – 2](../../media/06-photo-gemini-estimate/en-01-estimate.png)

3. Tap the green **Add** on a card. The entry goes into the selected meal as an estimate, with the photo. The star saves the dish as a favorite.

## Result

Estimates are approximate. If something is off, fix it with the next scenario. Without a key the photo only gets the on-device labels.
