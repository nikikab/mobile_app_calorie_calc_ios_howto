# 4. Search food and barcode

Find a product in Open Food Facts by name or barcode and add it with a portion.

**Prerequisites:** Internet connection.

## Steps

1. Tap **+** in the **Diary**. Pick the meal and type the name in **Product name**, then tap Search on the keyboard. Your favorites are listed under the field; tap one to add it again.

   ![Search food and barcode – 1](../../media/04-search-and-barcode/en-01-search-home.png)

2. Pick a product from the results. Each row shows calories per 100 g.

3. Enter the grams. Calories and macros are calculated. Tap **Add**.

4. For a barcode tap **Scan barcode** and point the camera at the code on the package. The first time iOS asks for camera permission; if you said no, allow it in the iPhone Settings app under KaloCalc → Camera.

## Result

If a search finds little or nothing and you have a Gemini key, the app translates it to French, searches again and returns the results in your language. If a barcode is not in the database you get a message and can add the food manually.
