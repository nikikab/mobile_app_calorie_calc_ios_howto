# 5. Food photo: offline labels

Snap the meal and the app suggests search terms with no internet and no key.

**Prerequisites:** The camera, or a photo in your library. KaloCalc never gets your whole library: it receives only the photo you pick.

## Steps

1. Tap the camera button in the **Diary**, or **Take food photo** / **Choose photo** on the **Search food** screen.

2. On-device recognition (Apple's Vision framework) suggests generic labels such as “salad” under “Recognized from the photo”. Tap a label to search with it.

   ![Food photo: offline labels – 2](../../media/05-photo-offline-labels/en-01-labels.png)

3. Pick a product and grams as in a normal search. The photo is saved with the entry.

## Result

The labels are generic, not an exact dish, and at this stage the photo never leaves the phone. For name, grams and calories see the next scenario.
